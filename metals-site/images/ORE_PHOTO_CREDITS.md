**Chemistry caption caution:** bauxite is a heterogeneous aluminium ore made mainly of aluminium hydroxide minerals, with other minerals and impurities; it is not a pure `Al₂O₃` specimen. Alumina (`Al₂O₃`) is refined from bauxite before electrolysis. [USGS source](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/bauxite-and-alumina-statistics-and-information).
