2.1 地球上的元素ELEMENTS ON EARTH

地殼裏，哪些元素最多？Which elements are most common in Earth’s crust?

地殼由多種元素組成。氧最多；含量最高的金屬是鋁。Earth’s crust contains many elements. Oxygen is the most abundant; aluminium is the most abundant metal.

2.2 元素由原子組成WHAT IS AN ELEMENT MADE OF?

一直切下去，會到達甚麼？What do we reach if we keep cutting?

把一塊銅不斷分成更小的部分，最後會到達組成銅的基本粒子：原子。If we keep dividing copper into smaller pieces, we reach its basic building block: an atom.

2.3 怎樣分類元素？CLASSIFYING ELEMENTS

看性質，分辨三種元素。Use properties to sort elements.

大部分元素是金屬。另有非金屬和類金屬。Most elements are metals. The other groups are non-metals and metalloids.

2.4 原子的基本結構BASIC STRUCTURE OF AN ATOM

很小的原子核，帶着原子的中心。A tiny nucleus sits at the centre.

原子核含有質子和中子。電子在原子核外快速移動；原子大部分是空間。The nucleus contains protons and neutrons. Electrons move outside it; most of an atom is empty space.

2.5 原子序和質量數ATOMIC NUMBER & MASS NUMBER

兩個數字，數清原子裏的粒子。Two numbers count the particles.

原子序是質子數；質量數是質子數加中子數。The atomic number counts protons. The mass number counts protons plus neutrons.

2.6 甚麼是同位素？WHAT ARE ISOTOPES?

同一種元素，質子數相同，中子數不同。Same protons, different neutrons.

同位素有相同原子序、不同質量數。它們的化學性質相同。Isotopes have the same atomic number but different mass numbers. Their chemical properties are the same.

2.7 相對同位素質量和相對原子質量RELATIVE ISOTOPIC & ATOMIC MASS

天然比例，決定平均質量。Natural abundance sets the average.

以碳-12 作為標準，質量定為 12。相對原子質量是天然同位素質量的加權平均。Carbon-12 is the standard, set at 12. Relative atomic mass is a weighted average of the masses of natural isotopes.

氯的天然同位素比例NATURAL CHLORINE ISOTOPES

35.5更常見的氯-35，令平均值更接近 35。More common chlorine-35 pulls the average closer to 35.

相對同位素質量約等於質量數；相對原子質量要同時考慮同位素質量和天然比例。Relative isotopic mass is about the mass number. Relative atomic mass depends on both isotope masses and their natural abundances.
